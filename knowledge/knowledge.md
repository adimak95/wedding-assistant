## Visa and entry

- Mexican citizens need a visa for India. They can apply online for an e-Visa (e-Tourist), which covers attending a wedding and sightseeing.
- The planner recommends the 1-year e-Tourist visa, because it can be applied for further in advance. The 30-day e-Tourist visa is also fine if it covers the travel dates; guests who already applied for it don't need to change anything.
- Apply ONLY on the official government site: https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/ — many look-alike sites charge extra fees.
- Minimum is 4 days before travel, but apply 3–4 weeks ahead to leave room for problems.
- Delhi airport accepts e-Visas. Bring a printed copy of the approval email. Fingerprints and a photo are taken on arrival.
- Tips from guests who applied: type names without accents (e.g. "Garcia", not "García") because the form rejects accented letters; if the form keeps sending you back after saving, look for fields marked in red.
- No email after 72 hours: check the application status on the e-Visa site using the temporary application number. The embassy cannot see e-Visa applications.
- e-Visa help desk (24/7): indiatvoa@gov.in or +91 11 2430 0666.
- Never quote a visa fee or passport rules as fact; they change. Point guests to the official site.

## Practical India info

- Health: no mandatory vaccines for travellers from Mexico. Hepatitis A and B or typhoid vaccines may be recommended. Yellow fever proof is only needed when arriving from a country where yellow fever is present. The couple suggests packing antihistamines. Guests should check https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel/destinations/traveler/none/india and talk to their doctor at least a month before travelling. The planner shared a one-page health and vaccination guide in the guest WhatsApp group.
- Phone and internet: buy an eSIM before travelling (for example Airalo or Holafly) and activate it on arrival.
- Money: Indian rupee (INR). Get cash at airport ATMs or exchange counters. Cards work at most hotels and restaurants; cash helps for small purchases and transport.
- Weather in mid-November: sunny days around 25–30°C, cooler mornings and evenings. Pack light clothing plus jeans or light layers.
- Air quality: Delhi's air is often poor in November (in November 2025 it averaged "very poor", with several "severe" days). Guests with asthma or heart or lung conditions should bring their medication and ask their doctor; N95 masks help outdoors.
